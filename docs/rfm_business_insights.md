## Key Findings

- Champions and Loyal Customers represent the highest-value customer groups.
- Potential Loyalists represent an important conversion opportunity.
- At-Risk customers should be targeted with retention and re-engagement campaigns.
- Hibernating customers have the lowest average customer value.

## Recommended Actions

1. Protect Champions through loyalty programs and exclusive benefits.
2. Increase engagement among Loyal Customers using personalized cross-sell offers.
3. Convert Potential Loyalists through targeted recommendations and incentives.
4. Re-engage At-Risk customers with personalized campaigns.
5. Use cost-efficient campaigns for Hibernating customers and avoid excessive discounting.
